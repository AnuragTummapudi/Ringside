Scenario: strong leverage
Use when the customer has long tenure, no contract, and a verified comparable offer.
Recommended approach: loyalty first, then comparable pricing, then retention review.

Scenario: best offer
If the representative says the available rate is final, ask once for a retention review and record the offer without inventing a competitor or policy.

Scenario: no deal
If no lower price is offered, mark the result as no_deal or best_offer and do not claim savings.
